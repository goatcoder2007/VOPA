# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Prospective parents/guardians researching faith-based K-12 education for their children
- Current enrolled families checking updates, events, and school information
- Community members and general public seeking information about the academy

## Product Purpose

Valley of Peace SDA Academy's website serves as the primary digital presence for the school. It communicates the school's identity, programs, and values to prospective and current families, and provides pathways to contact, apply, and stay informed. Success means visitors understand what the school offers and can take the next step — whether applying, contacting, or learning more.

## Positioning

A Seventh-day Adventist academy offering faith-based K-12 education with a focus on academic excellence, spiritual growth, and whole-student development. Established 2006.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive to research schools, find contact information, explore academic programs, or check school events. The site is the first impression for prospective families and a daily reference for current families.
